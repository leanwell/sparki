# Sparki Family of Products

Welcome to the **Sparki** ecosystem! This repository serves as the central showcase for the Sparki family of products, anchoring each project to its dedicated repository.

## Available Projects

Explore the current lineup of Sparki projects available in their respective repositories:

### <img src="assets/SPARKIHUB.png" height="30" align="top"> [SparkiHub](https://github.com/leanwell/sparkihub)
SparkiHub is the central command station for your home network, powered efficiently by a Raspberry Pi. It acts as a purely local, private cloud (also known as a NAS, or Network Attached Storage), keeping your data strictly inside your home rather than relying on external internet servers. It gives you a safe place to store and access all your files over your local network using our dedicated Windows and Android applications. But it doesn't stop at storage—it also seamlessly connects to your Bambu A1 3D printer and standard paper printer, letting you manage all your print jobs smoothly and wirelessly from one place.

### <img src="assets/sparki1000_longlogo.png" height="30" align="top"> [SPARKI1000](https://github.com/leanwell/SPARKI1000)
The SPARKI1000 is the ultimate smart desk companion designed to sit perfectly on your workspace. It keeps you informed at a glance with a clean display showing the current time, room temperature, and what you're currently listening to on Spotify. It's built to be a helpful, stylish, and functional addition to your daily workflow.
